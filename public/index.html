About the experiment

Individual lights send every report over TCP. In the aggregated route, lights send UDP reports to one gateway. It sends periodic TCP summaries and forwards faults immediately.

Each moving dot represents one received message, slowed for viewing. Amber marks a fault and a large green dot marks a summary.

Exhibition inspiration

At AIoT Korea 2026, the hyBee booth displayed 5.66 MB for individual LTE, 3.45 MB for gateway forwarding and 1.08 MB for its optimized configuration. This lab measures application payload on local sockets.

Source: Kim Si Hyung, AIoT Korea 2026 Exhibition Report, §4.
